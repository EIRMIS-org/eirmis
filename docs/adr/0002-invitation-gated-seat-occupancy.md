# Gate seat occupancy on accepted invitation status while keeping primary attendees immutable

When an `accepted` invitation transitions to `declined` (or any non-`accepted` status), we keep its Primary Attendee row immutable (`status = 'approved'`) to preserve `attendees_primary_must_be_approved` and audit history, and instead gate seat occupancy (`invitations.party_size` and Event Capacity / Approved Headcount calculations) on `invitations.status = 'accepted'`. Transitioning out of `accepted` resets any approved Additional Attendees back to `pending` (clearing review metadata) and clears `qr_token` across all attendees on the invitation, while check-in (`record_attendee_check_in`), attendee approval (`decide_attendee`), and headcount approval (`decide_headcount_request`) require `invitations.status = 'accepted'`. This avoids deleting or weakening Primary Attendee invariants while ensuring declined invitations free all seats for Waitlist Promotion, invalidate old QR codes, and consume exactly one seat if the guest later re-accepts or is promoted from the waitlist.
